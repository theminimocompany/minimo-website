A compact loft reborn through warm plywood, raw steel, and quiet restraint. Every surface was considered, every joint exposed, every shadow intentional.

Hong Kong · 2025 · 690 sq ft

Great Design Must Be Honest

Honest about the way it is built — every joint, every material choice, every structural decision laid bare rather than concealed behind false finishes. Honest about how it fits into the history of the building, respecting what was already there while making it genuinely livable for today. And honest about the people who will inhabit it, creating spaces that serve real life rather than impose a fantasy.

At The Minimo Company, we don't decorate. We listen — to the light, to the existing structure, to the way you actually move through a room — then we edit, refine, and build with restraint until only what matters remains. The result isn't a stage set. It's a quiet backdrop for daily life.
